## Chapter 1: Electrical Foundations

Welcome back to electrical principles! You already know voltage, current, and resistance from your Technician studies. Now we're going to expand that foundation with the AC circuit concepts that make HF radio work.

We will learn about reactance, the opposition to AC that changes with frequency. Reactance and resistance combine to form impedance, which helps determine how your antenna system behaves. From antenna tuners to crystal filters, you'll see resonance at work. Transformers let us match different impedances. We'll also cover peak envelope power (PEP) and the decibels hams use to compare signal levels.

We’ll start with series and parallel circuits, the building blocks of an antenna matching network. Then we’ll see why the match changes as you tune across a band.

We'll explain terms like "inductive reactance" and "impedance transformation" step by step, using practical radio examples throughout.
