# How to Use This HamBook

## What's Inside

This HamBook covers the US General class (Element 3) exam pool valid from 2023 to 2027. You can find the latest version of the book at [hambook.org](https://hambook.org). We focus on:

- **Deeper Technical Concepts**: We build on your Technician knowledge to explain circuits, components, and signals in more detail.
- **HF Operating**: Practical guidance for operating on the worldwide bands, including propagation, antenna tuning, and etiquette.
- **Regulations**: The rules specific to General class privileges and HF operation.
- **Safety**: Electrical and RF safety, including precautions for higher power levels and different station setups.
- **Question Reviews**: Questions and answers from the actual exam pool, grouped with the topics that explain them.

## Structure of the HamBook

### Part 1: Expanding on Radio Science {#how-to-use-part-1}

[Part 1]({{% pageref "pt1" %}}) follows a signal from the circuits in your radio through the antenna and out into the ionosphere. If you have a strong background in radio and electronics, much of Part 1 may feel familiar.

If you remember resistors and capacitors from the Technician exam, you'll now see how they work together in a filter. You'll also build on what you know about antennas by learning how to match one to its feed line.

### Part 2: Expanding on Radio Operation {#how-to-use-part-2}

[Part 2]({{% pageref "pt2" %}}) takes you into the station to set up safely, get on the air, and learn which frequencies and modes your license lets you use. Even if you've studied electronics or worked in commercial radio, some amateur operating practices may be new to you.

## Study Strategies

If your Technician knowledge is rusty, or you want **deeper study**, follow the explanations, diagrams, and worked examples in order. Then try the section questions with the answers covered. If you're still puzzling over something practical, like how to tune an antenna, ask an experienced ham to show you.

For a **quick exam review**, start with the **Key Information** boxes and *italicized exam facts*. If you recently passed Technician, the questions at the end of each section can help you spot what's new. When an answer surprises you, go back to the related explanation and try again.

{{< web-only >}}At the end of each section, the **Open in HamStudy** link opens a study session for that section's questions.{{< /web-only >}}

{{< print-only >}}The QR codes at the end of sections open a study session for the questions you've just covered. Scan one with your phone to review that material in HamStudy.{{< /print-only >}}

HamStudy.org offers free question study and practice exams. The mobile app also lets you study offline.

### Working Through the Math

Some questions ask you to use a formula, convert units, or calculate values such as resistance and power. The book includes worked examples so you can follow each step.

Some math helps answer pool questions, and some explains why a concept works. Brief bold notes mark extra math you can skim.

Only one question comes from each pool group, so several related calculations may account for just one question on your exam. If you're stuck, give yourself permission to move on. You can come back to that example when you've had a break.

### Final Exam Preparation

Once you've covered the material, use this study cycle:

1. Use Study Mode until you've seen every question at least once.
2. Take a practice exam to identify gaps.
3. Use "Study Weakest Areas" in the app, or select the categories where you missed questions on the website. Read the related explanations here when you need them.
4. Study those areas, then take another practice exam.

Consistently scoring 85% or better gives you a useful margin above the 26-out-of-35 passing score.

You can also keep this book as a reference after passing. When you're ready to try a new mode or put up your first HF antenna, the contents will take you back to the relevant chapter.
