# Appendix

Use the [phonetic alphabet]({{% pageref "phonetics" %}}) to practice spelling call signs clearly. The [question pool]({{% pageref "/appendx/2023-2027-general-class-question-pool" %}}) lets you review individual questions by ID.

For updates and further study, these sources are useful:

- [HamBook.org](https://hambook.org): the latest version of this book.
- [NCVEC General question pool](https://ncvec.org/index.php/2023-2027-general-question-pool-release): the official pool and its corrections.
- [FCC amateur radio rules, Part 97](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/chapter-I/subchapter-D/part-97): current rules for US amateur operation, available at eCFR.gov.
- [HamStudy.org](https://hamstudy.org/): question study and practice exams.

Use the rules that apply on the day you operate. A printed band chart or an answer in an older study guide may no longer reflect them.
