## Why this order

- Components and logic introduce practical device behavior. AC phase, impedance, and resonance then prepare readers for complete circuits.
- Normal mixing and modulation come before receiver images and overload. Direct sampling is defined before its bandwidth limits; converters precede DSP and DDS.
- Wire antennas lead to loading and arrays. Matching methods and Smith charts stay together; receiving antennas then apply the earlier pattern and noise models.
- Ground and tropospheric paths precede ionospheric paths and space weather. Meteor scatter and aurora use those foundations; lunar reflection introduces a different path.
- Safety precedes station work. Receiver controls and interference remedies come before deeper instrument checks; transmitter checks prepare readers for on-air operation.
- JT65 properties sit beside the Q65 comparison. Satellite permissions accompany the link choices they constrain.
- The rules chapter applies earlier signal and station concepts. Operator authority and permitted messages have separate sections; exam administration is a new responsibility at the end.

## Scope decisions

- The two-part theory/application division comes from the brief. No other book outline serves as a template. Earlier-pool references guide refresher depth and familiar terminology, not chapter placement.
- Technician and General topics receive short refreshers where an Extra question needs them; reading the earlier HamBooks is never a prerequisite.
- Station power stays a short two-question lesson. The 15-question antenna-pattern lesson reuses plots and calculation methods; raw question count is not teaching load.
- Ground/tropospheric paths and scatter/moonbounce now have separate lessons (6 and 7 questions). Control authority and permitted messages also split by task (8 and 8).
- Television remains with signal theory because scanning and coding apply the preceding signal models. Its receiver choices are brief applications of those formats.
- Question counts measure coverage, not eventual page count. All chapter boundaries remain open to evidence about teaching load; no section is padded for symmetry.
