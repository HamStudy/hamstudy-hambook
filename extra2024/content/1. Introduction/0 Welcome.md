# Welcome to Amateur Extra

Welcome to the HamStudy Amateur Extra Class License HamBook! Perhaps a contact just outside your General frequency privileges has you thinking about an upgrade. You may also want to understand what happens inside your radio, improve a station, help others get licensed, or earn the highest license class. Whatever brought you here, let's give those exam answers something to connect to.

You don't need to be an electronics engineer or an expert operator to begin. You will need the foundations covered by the Technician and General exams. If you haven't studied those yet, start with the corresponding HamBooks at [hambook.org](https://hambook.org). You can take more than one exam at the same session if you're ready; earning the higher license still requires credit for the lower exams.

You can use this book whether or not you've read either earlier HamBook. We'll include reminders where a new idea depends on an older one.

## What's Inside This Book

The Extra exam reaches further into how radio works. We'll cover:

- **Circuits and components:** How timing, stored energy, and component behavior shape a signal.
- **Signals and receivers:** How radios carry information, process it digitally, and separate wanted signals from noise.
- **Antennas and propagation:** How antenna systems direct energy and how signals travel between stations.
- **Station work and operating:** Safe installation, useful measurements, interference, satellites, weak-signal contacts, and more.
- **Rules and exam questions:** The responsibilities that come with your privileges, with the actual pool questions beside the topics that teach them.

HF operating is part of the story, but there's plenty here for interests closer to home—or farther out, through satellites and moonbounce. You don't have to pursue every activity to earn the license. As you study, some topics will be useful right away; others may give you an idea for something to try later.

This edition covers the US Amateur Extra question pool valid from July 1, 2024, through June 30, 2028. Pools and rules change, so visit [hambook.org](https://hambook.org) for the latest version of the book.
