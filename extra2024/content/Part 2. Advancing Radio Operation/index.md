# Part 2: Advancing Radio Operation

A new antenna may change where people can safely stand while you transmit. A receiver adjustment may hide interference without removing its source. Putting the earlier theory to work helps us check those effects and choose the next step.

We begin with the hazards to address before installing or testing equipment, then use station tools to measure performance and solve problems. Operating techniques and regulations round out the picture, from a fast contest exchange to a satellite contact or an exam session you're helping administer.

Some activities may already be part of your operating routine; others may be reasons you want to upgrade. A puzzling noise or a disappointing antenna test gives the theory a setting beyond the exam. The aim is to turn what you can explain into decisions you can make at the station.
