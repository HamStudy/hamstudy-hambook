# Part 2: Advancing Radio Operation

Knowing how a radio works helps when the station doesn't behave as expected. Is a weak signal really weak, or is a strong neighbor overloading the receiver? Does an antenna adjustment improve the match, and how can you measure the difference?

This part puts the earlier ideas to work. We begin with the hazards to address before installing or testing equipment, then use station tools to measure performance and solve problems. Operating techniques and regulations round out the picture, from a fast contest exchange to a satellite contact or an exam session you're helping administer.

You may already do some of these things with your current license. Bring that experience along. A puzzling noise or a disappointing antenna test can give the theory a much more memorable setting than an exam question does.
