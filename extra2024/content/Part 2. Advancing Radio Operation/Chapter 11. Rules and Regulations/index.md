## Chapter 11: Rules and Regulations

The Extra upgrade gives you more room on the dial. Using that room well means checking more than the displayed frequency: the whole signal must fit, the site must be suitable, and the message must be permitted. A few concrete questions make the rules easier to apply. Where does the sideband end? Who can stop a remote transmitter? Who is responsible for a message that software forwards?

Operating across borders adds the host country's rules. Helping run an exam adds a responsibility to verify another operator's qualifications. Both call for the same habit: identify the permission you need and the conditions attached to it.

Rules and exam pools can change; consult the [current FCC Part 97 rules](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/chapter-I/subchapter-D/part-97) and the [latest HamBook at hambook.org](https://hambook.org) for updates.
