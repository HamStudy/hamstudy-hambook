## Chapter 11: Rules and Regulations

The Extra upgrade expands your operating privileges without changing the need to fit the complete signal within them. Near a segment edge, that means calculating where the sideband ends. At a remote station, it means providing a way to stop the transmitter even when its control link fails. Each operating choice brings particular conditions to check.

This chapter applies the rules to signal limits, shared spectrum, station sites, and message handling. Travel and exam administration add other responsibilities: establish which permission covers the station, or verify that a candidate has met the requirements for a license. The useful distinction is between having permission for an activity and meeting all the conditions attached to it.

Rules and exam pools can change; consult the [current FCC Part 97 rules](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-47/chapter-I/subchapter-D/part-97) and the [latest HamBook at hambook.org](https://hambook.org) for updates.
