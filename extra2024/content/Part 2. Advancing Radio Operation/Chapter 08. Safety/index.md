## Chapter 8: Safety

A station plan has to account for more than signal strength. It also needs safe ways to install and service antennas, handle surges on incoming cables, and keep people clear of excessive RF exposure. Make those decisions before work begins.

This chapter focuses on the safety topics added by the Extra pool: tower attachments, coordinated surge protection, and RF exposure at your station and nearby. The basic precautions still apply: keep antennas clear of power lines and treat stored electrical energy with care.
