## Chapter 8: Safety

You've worked out how to put more signal in the right direction. Before installing that antenna, there's another set of questions: who will put it up, where can people approach it, and what happens when a surge reaches its cables? Safety belongs in the station plan from the start.

This chapter focuses on the safety topics added by the Extra pool: tower attachments, coordinated surge protection, and RF exposure at your station and nearby. The earlier precautions still apply, including keeping antennas clear of power lines and treating stored electrical energy with care. An upgrade adds privileges; it doesn't remove hazards.
