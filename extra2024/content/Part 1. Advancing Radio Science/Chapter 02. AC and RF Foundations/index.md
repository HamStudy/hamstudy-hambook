## Chapter 2: AC and RF Foundations

Changing a coil's core or a tuning diode's voltage changes how a circuit behaves. The previous chapter showed those effects; now we'll learn how to predict them. A charging time, a phase angle, or a resonant frequency puts a number to something happening in the radio.

We'll start with voltage and current on a graph, then use that picture to calculate what happens in a circuit. Keep a calculator nearby and work through one operation at a time. Each answer describes something you can measure, and the drawings give you another way to check it. Even the stray inductance of a component lead will have a place in the model.
