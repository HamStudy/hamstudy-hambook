## Chapter 2: AC and RF Foundations

A circuit may need to settle within a certain time, match a load, or select a narrow range of frequencies. Resistance, capacitance, and inductance determine those results. This chapter puts numbers to them: charging time, impedance, phase angle, resonant frequency, and bandwidth.

We'll use graphs to connect phase and energy storage, then turn those pictures into calculations. Keep a calculator nearby and work through one operation at a time. Each answer describes something you can measure, and the drawings give you another way to check it. Even the stray inductance of a component lead will have a place in the model.
